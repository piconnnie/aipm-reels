Most meeting notes record what was said. The decisions are buried in the middle, often without an owner.

The prompt (paste your notes first):
“From these notes, list only decisions actually made. For each: decision, owner, due date, open question. If no owner: write UNASSIGNED. Don’t invent anything.”

Why it works:
→ “only decisions” filters out discussion
→ UNASSIGNED makes missing owners visible instead of guessed
→ “don’t invent anything” keeps it to what the notes say

Check the output against the notes before you send it. The example in the reel is made up.
