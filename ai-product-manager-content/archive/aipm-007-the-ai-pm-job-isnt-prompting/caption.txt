Prompts change every week. The definition of “good” is the part of the job that lasts.

What an AI PM actually owns:
→ the eval set: real inputs and what a good answer looks like
→ the failure budget: which mistakes are never acceptable
→ the failure design: what users see when the model is wrong or unsure

Prompting matters, and engineers often do it better. Deciding what “good” means is the PM’s call.
