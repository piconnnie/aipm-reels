A confident, friendly wrong answer often gets a thumbs up.

That’s why satisfaction and task success can move in opposite directions. Users rate how an answer feels; they often can’t tell whether it was right.

How to choose:
→ lead with task success when the job has a checkable outcome (booked, resolved, correct)
→ lead with satisfaction when quality is a matter of taste (tone, style)
→ track both, and treat a gap between them as a bug report

(The 82% vs 54% in the reel is an illustrative example, not data from a real product.)
