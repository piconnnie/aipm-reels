Using a model to score your model’s answers is fast. It’s only useful if it agrees with the people who define “good”.

Before trusting an LLM judge:
→ take a sample of real answers (50+ is a reasonable start)
→ score them with humans and with the judge
→ check agreement, especially on failures that matter, like confident made-up details

Re-check whenever you change the judge’s prompt or model. (The three example rows in the reel are illustrative.)

Save this for your eval setup.
