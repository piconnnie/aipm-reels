An agent can reach the right answer the wrong way, and a final-answer eval will score it a pass.

What a final-answer check misses:
→ the wrong tool, or made-up arguments, that happened to work
→ fourteen steps for a three-step job (cost and latency)
→ an action nobody approved, like sending an email

Grade the trajectory too:
→ tool choice and arguments
→ step count against what you expect
→ any write or external action without approval = fail

(The three runs in the reel are illustrative examples.)
