Some of the best AI product decisions are “no AI here”.

Use AI only if:
→ a fixed rule can’t do the job well
→ occasional errors are acceptable
→ a wrong answer can be caught or undone
→ you have real data to test it on

Fail two of the four? Ship the rule-based version first and revisit with data.

Save this for your next roadmap review.
