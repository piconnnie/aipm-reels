A PoC runs a few people, short prompts and one call per question. Production runs none of those.

What multiplies between the demo and the launch:
→ volume: every user, every day
→ context: retrieved documents and chat history make prompts longer
→ retries and tool calls: one task can mean several model calls
→ eval runs: you re-test on every prompt or model change

Model this before the business case goes in. The unit that matters to finance is cost per completed task, not cost per call.

Save this before you scale a PoC.
