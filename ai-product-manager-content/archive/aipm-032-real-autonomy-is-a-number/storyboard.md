# Storyboard — aipm-032-real-autonomy-is-a-number

**Topic:** How to measure real autonomy instead of marketing autonomy  
**Pillar:** C: Agentic AI · **Format:** framework · **Objective:** saves · **Depth:** intermediate  
**Audience:** PMs and buyers evaluating AI agents  
**Duration:** 24.8s @ 30fps · 1080×1920 · **Voiceover:** off

**Core insight (one sentence):** An agent’s real autonomy is measured by how often a human has to step in per task and how much time each intervention costs, not by the label on the product.
