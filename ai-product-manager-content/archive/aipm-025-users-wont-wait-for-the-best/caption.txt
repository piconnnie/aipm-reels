A slightly better answer that arrives much later can be the worse product.

A smaller, faster model is often the right call when:
→ the task is narrow: classify, extract, rewrite, route
→ your eval set shows no meaningful quality drop
→ a person is waiting on the answer
→ a larger model is still there for the hard cases

Route by difficulty, measure both speed and quality, and decide with your own eval set rather than a public leaderboard.

(The 8 s vs 2 s in the reel is an illustrative example, not a benchmark.)
