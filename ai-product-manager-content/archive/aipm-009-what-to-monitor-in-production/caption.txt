An LLM feature rarely breaks loudly. It drifts.

Five signals to watch from day one:
→ input drift (new kinds of questions)
→ refusal / “not sure” rate
→ cost per completed task
→ user corrections and retries
→ eval pass rate on every prompt or model change

Pick a threshold for each before launch, so a change triggers a look rather than a debate.

Save this for your launch checklist.
