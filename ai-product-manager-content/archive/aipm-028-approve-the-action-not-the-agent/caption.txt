“Human in the loop” for an agent shouldn’t mean approving every step. Gate the actions instead.

A simple gate map:
→ Read-only lookups: no gate, just log them
→ Drafts and internal notes: spot-check later
→ Messages to customers: approve before sending
→ Money, deletes, permissions: always approve

The rule: the harder an action is to undo, the earlier a human steps in. List every tool your agent can call and give each one a gate level before launch.
