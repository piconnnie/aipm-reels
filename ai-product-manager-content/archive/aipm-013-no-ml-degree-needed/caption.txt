Moving into AI product management doesn’t start with training models.

The skills that show up in the work:
→ writing a clear eval set: real inputs and what good looks like
→ reasoning about error costs: which mistakes matter and which don’t
→ explaining trade-offs simply to engineers and executives

Technical fluency helps. You build it by working with the team, not before you’re allowed in the room.
