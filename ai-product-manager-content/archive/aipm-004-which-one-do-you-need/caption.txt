“Should we fine-tune?” is usually the wrong first question. Ask what the model is missing.

Missing knowledge (private docs, facts that change every week) → retrieval. Put the right page in front of the model at answer time.

Missing behaviour (a format, a house tone, a narrow skill) → fine-tuning can help.

Ignoring clear instructions → fix the prompt first. It’s the cheapest experiment, and many teams skip it.

Plenty of products end up using retrieval and fine-tuning together. Whichever you try, measure it on the same eval set before and after.
