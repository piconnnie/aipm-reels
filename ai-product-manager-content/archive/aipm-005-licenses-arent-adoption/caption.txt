A licence tells you someone can use the tool. It doesn’t tell you they do — or that it helps.

Common rollout metrics measure access:
→ licences assigned
→ logins this month
→ prompts sent

Better signals of adoption and value:
→ weekly users on a real, named task
→ tasks done faster or better (pick one task, measure before and after)
→ people still using it after week four

(The 100% vs 18% in the reel is an illustrative example, not data from a real organisation.)
