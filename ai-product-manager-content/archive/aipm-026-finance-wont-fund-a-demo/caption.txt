A great demo answers “can it work?” Finance is asking “what changes, at what cost, and when do we know?”

Five boxes on one page:
→ Baseline: how the task performs today, measured
→ Change: the one number AI should move
→ Cost envelope: run cost at full volume, not PoC volume
→ Risk: what happens when it’s wrong, and who catches it
→ Decision date: when you stop or scale

Start with the baseline. If nobody has measured the task today, measure that first.

(The invoice-triage numbers in the reel are an illustrative example.)
