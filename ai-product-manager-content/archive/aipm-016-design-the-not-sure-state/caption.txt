An assistant that never says “not sure” will eventually be confidently wrong in front of a user.

Design the unsure state before launch:
→ say so plainly, in one line
→ show what it did find, with sources
→ offer the next step or a human

Then track how often it happens. Rising “not sure” rates are an early signal; a rate near zero can mean it’s guessing.
