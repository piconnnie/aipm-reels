A normal PRD template quietly assumes the software does what it’s told. AI features don’t.

Four sections to add to every AI PRD:
→ Failure modes: what a wrong answer looks like, and the fallback
→ Eval plan: the test set, the metric, the pass bar
→ Data: what it reads, who owns it, how fresh it must be
→ Cost envelope: maximum cost and latency per task

Write them before the build starts. A blank section is a question the team will answer by accident later.

Save this before your next AI PRD.
