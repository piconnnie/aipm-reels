“Keep a human in the loop” is where the design starts, not where it ends.

Four settings for human review:
→ Review all: a person approves every output
→ Review risky: only flagged or high-stakes cases
→ Sample: check a random slice each week
→ Audit: look at logs after incidents

The metric that moves the dial: how often reviewers change what the AI produced. When that stays low on a task, move that task down one level. When it rises, move it back up.

Set it per task. A refund promise and a greeting don’t deserve the same review.
